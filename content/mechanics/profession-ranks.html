The four ranks

Type a level into any character card and the ranks you have unlocked turn green.

Why the level half is the one that matters

Skill arrives early, level arrives late. A profession levelled alongside questing will sit at its cap waiting for the character to catch up, not the other way round. Expert needs level 20 and caps you at 225; Artisan needs level 35. Plan the trip to the trainer around the level, and treat the skill number as something that will already be there.
