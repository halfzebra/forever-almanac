How a camp works

objects
Three per profession. The first arrives at around 20 skill from the trainer; the second and third are upgraded from dungeon blueprints.
capacity
Three objects per basic campfire, and one contribution per player, so a camp is a group effort. Cooking blueprints at 90 and 200 raise the capacity to 5 and 10.
duration
An hour, on a shared hour-long cooldown. So the choice of which object to place is a real one, not something you redo per pull.
the catch
They do not stack with the equivalent class buff. An object whose buff your group already has is simply a wasted slot. This is the rule worth internalising.

Every object, and what cancels it

The clash column names the class buff that overrides the object. Each character page works out which of these matter for that character and which to place.

The clash depends on when you learn the buff, not just whether you have it. A priest's Faction Banner is useful until she learns Divine Spirit at 30, and dead afterwards; her First Aid Kit is dead from level 1, because Power Word: Fortitude is. The character pages compute that rather than assume it.
