Professions

first aid
Crafts healing potions now. They moved off Alchemy, which gets a worse version. For a cloth wearer this makes First Aid considerably more than a bandage skill.
camping
Every profession grants three camp objects. Entirely new. The camp has the rules and the clash table.
merchant's favor
Earned from loot crates, and buys recipes outright. Which means some recipes are never trained at all, and a crate turn-in is worth planning crafts around.
tailoring
No confirmed specializations. Mooncloth and Shadoweave survive only as bag and Craftsman's Writ names. Leatherworking does keep Dragonscale, Elemental and Tribal.

Talents and abilities

The trees are reworked, not restored. Efficient Strikes, Coup de Grace, Power in Light, Twilight Focus, Dark Inspiration, Boot Knife and Death Mark are new. Penance, Divine Aegis and Renewed Hope were imported from later expansions. Holy Precision sits in Discipline despite the name. Rank counts changed too: Wand Specialization is 2 ranks for the full +25%, Dual Wield Specialization 2 ranks for +50%.
New abilities exist outside the trees, and talents key off them. Shank, at rogue level 24, has three talents built around it - Flash of Steel, Venom and Boot Knife - so five points of a Combat build serve an ability the talent guides barely mention. Worth checking for the same pattern on any class before writing a build up.
Dual spec at level 40. Blizzard's own words: "Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40". The quote sits in the Mage and Shaman deep dive, and it reaches us through Zockify rather than from the article itself, which is the caveat that belongs with it. The arithmetic covers the rest.
