The arithmetic

points
Level minus 9. First point at level 10, one per level after, so 51 at level 60. Subtract what your sheet actually has from that number before you believe any respec advice - it is what caught two unaccounted points on the priest at level 23.
rows
Row N opens at 5 × (N − 1) points in that tree. Row 1 is free, row 5 needs 20, row 7 needs 30. In that tree, not in total, which is the mistake most build arguments turn out to be.
dual spec
Level 40. Which is why a character with two viable specs does not have to choose. See the caveat below on where that quote comes from.
respec cost
Nobody publishes it. Only the Legacy respec tiers have been datamined, and the site that published them says they need live confirmation. Ask a trainer.

Two traps

Both are cases where a source names a prerequisite without mentioning what it costs you.

Trace the prerequisite before you take the swap. Priest Discipline runs Improved Power Word: Shield → Soul Warding → Renewed Hope, and separately Penance → Power Infusion. One guide offers Renewed Hope instead of Penance at 30 as a fair swap, without mentioning that it closes off the row 7 capstone permanently.
Check the build actually reaches the talent you are gearing for. Spiritual Guidance converts up to 25% of Spirit into healing power, which looks like the obvious partner to Spirit-heavy tailored cloth. It sits in Holy row 5, and a Discipline build's 13 Holy points open rows 1 and 2 only. Neither priest build here reaches it.
