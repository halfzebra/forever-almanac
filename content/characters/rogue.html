What's worth making for a rogue

None of the Forever guides rank items by class, so the sets below are what exists at those skill levels rather than a published rogue recommendation.

any skill
Armor Kits — Light, Heavy, Thick and Rugged, applied to chest, legs, hands or feet. The Mystic and Forceful versions are new in Forever and add Attack Power or Spell Power on top of armor. No source says which of the two gives Attack Power, so read the tooltip before you buy the pattern.
165–242
Dusky and Nightscape lines — leather you can wear while you level through it, which is the whole advantage you have over the priest's tailoring.
200–250
Merchant's Favor sets — Skulker's, Stalker's, Prowler's, Skirmisher's and others, 60 or 90 favor per pattern from Daniel Stitchsong in Redridge Mountains. The names suggest agility wearers but no guide confirms the stats.
242–296
Blue Suede Gloves, Wicked Leather Boots, Blue Suede Hat — all new in Forever, all auction-house patterns, and all on the leveling route anyway.
300
Grimstitch Armor — the rogue raid set. Shoulders, gloves, legs and boots are craftable, each needing Malleable Essence of Nature from disenchanting raid gear. Pieces bind on equip, so they're tradeable.
mount
Custom saddle — up to 5% mounted speed at max level, which is new in Forever and purely a leatherworker thing.

What this character brings the group

Both of your professions produce camp objects, and the rogue's are the ones the other two actually want.

Leatherworking objects

Camp Tent · skill 205 Light Leather, 1 silver from the trainer. Gives rested XP, up to 5% of a level, to everyone sitting nearby. This is the best thing you can do for your priest and warrior, and you can do it at level 10.
Tanning Rack · skill 125Tier 2. Only one source gives the skill level.
Sewing Machine · skill 300Tier 3, blueprint drop.

Skinning objects

Camp Chair · skill 202% critical strike to all spells and attacks for everyone nearby. Does not stack with Moonkin Aura. Learned from the trainer.
Field Guide · skill 1402% crit plus Track Beasts for whoever reads it. Blueprint drop; no source names the boss.
Trapper's Workbench · skill 3002% crit plus a trap. Which traps is still unclear.
Trade across your own characters. The priest enchants your gloves for +5 or +10 skinning and saves you Runecloth for Runic Leather Bracers; you send back Camp Tents, Armor Kits for the warrior's gear, and the leather she can't gather. A camp holds three objects and each player contributes one, so between the three of you a full camp is realistic.
