Talents: Combat, and you are on route already

You are 10 with one point spent, which is the cleanest possible start. Method recommends Combat for solo players and Subtlety only if you are grouped; wowhandbook recommends Combat and publishes a route that never needs a respec all the way to 60. You level alone, you skin everything you kill, and Blade Flurry turns multi-mob pulls into faster skinning — Combat it is.

Your first point should be Efficient Strikes. Check that it is. wowhandbook, talentsforever and classicwowforever all start Combat with Efficient Strikes 1/2 at level 10, so this is the one talent in the game that three sites agree on without qualification.
