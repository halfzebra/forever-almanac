Talents: respec, then pure Discipline to 40

You duo with a warrior, so you are the healer, and Discipline gets you there fastest. Shadowform needs 31 points in Shadow Magic and cannot be reached before level 40 no matter what you do, so going Discipline first costs you nothing in the Shadow tree — and dual spec reportedly opens at 40, at which point you stop choosing.

Your sheet needs a respec, and the maths doesn't add up either. You listed 2/2 Wand Specialization, 3/5 Shadow Focus, 5/5 Blackout and 2/2 Improved Shadow Word: Pain. That is 12 points, but level 23 grants 14 — your first point comes at 10 — so two points are somewhere you didn't mention. Check the sheet before you respec, because they might be the two you want to keep.
Wand Specialization 2/2Discipline, row 1Keep. Forever cut it from five ranks to two for the same +25%, so it is the cheapest damage on the page and it is already in the right tree.
Blackout 5/5Shadow, row 1The worst five points on your sheet. No guide I could find takes Blackout at all — it is absent from classicwowforever's Shadow route and from Maxroll's Shadow Magic list.
Shadow Focus 3/5Shadow, row 1A real Shadow-damage talent and dead weight to a healer. No Forever source publishes its current tooltip, so what it actually does now is unverified.
Improved SW: Pain 2/2Shadow, row 2On the Shadow route, not this one. Keep casting the DoT though — Power in Light boosts Smite and Penance against anything carrying Shadow Word: Pain or Holy Fire, so the spell stays in the rotation even when the talent points don't.
Nobody publishes what a respec costs in Forever. Not Wowhead, not Zockify. The only numbers anywhere are for resetting Legacy talents, a different system: 10, 20, 40, 100, 150 gold and up, dropping one tier every seven days — and Zockify says plainly that both the higher tiers and the decay still need confirming on live. There is also an unused perk in the client called Adaptability that resets class talents on a 4-hour cooldown, but it isn't active, so don't plan around it. Ask your trainer what it costs before you commit.

Run Discipline when the warrior is online and Shadow when you are not. That is the whole answer, and dual spec at 40 means you stop choosing. Maxroll is blunt that Discipline questing alone is "very slow" with no AoE, and says outright: "It is highly recommended to level as Shadow Magic instead to make solo questing a lot faster." Both full ladders are below — same 51 points, same level gates, different tree.
Good news about your existing points, if you lead with Shadow instead. Most of them are already on the Shadow route. Spirit Tap 5/5 is the very first thing it takes (10–14), Improved Shadow Word: Pain 2/2 lands at 15–16, and Shadow Focus climbs to 5/5 by 45. Only Blackout 5/5 is dead weight in every build on this page. So Shadow-first is the cheaper respec for you; Discipline-first is the better duo. Your call, and the sheet assessment above still applies either way.

What's actually worth making for a priest

Forever added a lot here, and the generic 1–300 guides skip all of it.

levels 12–50
Pristine and Gilded cloth sets — Forever's leveling cloth comes in six spell-bonus versions. Pristine covers 12–25, Gilded covers 30–50, and Nethergeld shoulders and cuffs cover 42–45. Most are bind-on-pickup, so you craft them for yourself. This is the one part of tailoring that directly helps you level.
ench 140
Insight, Recovery, Revelation — weapon enchants bought with Merchant's Favor, 45 each. Insight doubles your Spirit for 10 seconds, which is the one aimed squarely at you.
ench 210
Healing Power necklace — +11 healing and +4 spell power, also a Merchant's Favor recipe. Necklace enchants are new in Forever.
ench 300
Wisdom off-hand (+6 Intellect, +5 Spirit) and Mighty Healing Power for two-handers — up to +100 healing and +34 spell damage, 240 Merchant's Favor.
tailor 300
Robe of the Archmage and Truefaith Vestments — both now need new Alchemy-crafted dyes, and the 300 raid sets need Malleable Essence of Nature. Worth knowing before you promise anyone a robe. Your healing raid set is Vestments of Conviction.
bags
Reagent bags — Linen 6 slots up through Runecloth 14, Mooncloth 18, Bottomless 20. The single most appreciated thing a tailor makes for a duo partner.
favor
Merchant's Favor — earned by filling loot crates and turning them in at the Azeroth Commerce Authority camp, Three Corners in Redridge Mountains, around 10, 73. Several of the best enchants above are bought with it, not trained, so start collecting crates now.
Do not plan around tailoring specializations. There is no confirmed Mooncloth, Shadoweave or Spellfire specialization in Forever — those words appear only as bag names and Craftsman's Writ items. The Burning Crusade rule people remember (level 60, 350 skill, pick a branch) does not apply here.

For the warrior

He supplies your disenchant fodder and escorts you to Annora. Pay him in enchants.

Greater Strength bracer, Greater Impact 2H, Greater Striking weapon, Stats chest — all from Annora, all solid while leveling.
ench 290
Strength +25 on a two-hander — the one he'll actually want at 60.
tailoring
Bags, and nothing else — he wears mail and plate, so your cloth is disenchant fodder, not gear for him.
