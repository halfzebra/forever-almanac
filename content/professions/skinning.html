The one rule that governs skinning

Your skinning skill sets the highest creature level you can skin. Below skill 100 it's generous; above 100 it's exactly five times the creature's level, which means skill 300 is required for level 60 creatures.

So skin low, early. The failure mode is reaching a new zone and finding nothing there is skinnable. Clear the easy creatures in the zone you're leaving before you move on, because they give skill-ups you'll need to touch the next zone's animals at all. The formula here is published as a rule of thumb, not an exact table, and one source's own examples don't quite fit it — treat the numbers as close, not precise.

Where to skin, 1 to 300

Alliance and Night Elf friendly, following the zones you'd quest through anyway. Skill-ups don't depend on the zone being hard, only on the creature being skinnable at your skill.

Skinning gear stacks up. Keen Skinning Knife +3, Cartilage Shapers +4, Enchant Gloves – Skinning +5, or Advanced Skinning +10, which is new in Forever. That's up to 17 extra skill, which is the difference between skinning a zone and walking past it. Nobody publishes which enchanting skill Advanced Skinning needs — two sources guessed 250 and 270 and neither confirmed it, so have your priest check her trainer.
