Buy the rods before you need them

Silver at 109, Golden at 150, Truesilver at 200. They are blacksmithing items, so if nobody in the group is a blacksmith they come off the auction house - and they are the one thing that will stop the route dead.

Annora is inside Uldaman. She is the only Artisan enchanter for either faction, she only spawns once her area is cleared, and the 230–250 formulas exist nowhere else. Buy everything in one visit.

Trainers worth walking to

Enchanting, 1 to 300

Dust-only lines are the cheap ones: one or two mats a point and nothing from a vendor. Oils level faster but need vials and herbs. Forever added new low-level recipes, so the first 70 points are cheaper here than in Classic.

The rods catch people out. The plain Silver, Golden, Truesilver and Arcanite rods are blacksmithing items, and neither of you is a blacksmith. Buy each one off the auction house before you need it — at 81 your next one is the Silver Rod at 109, which is close, and being stuck waiting on a rod wastes days. Right-clicking a Greater Magic Essence splits it into three Lesser, which is worth remembering when a route calls for Lesser.
