Why it is cheap to level

Forever gives tailors no passive bonus — no increased cloth drop rate — so the saving comes entirely from what you do with the output.

Loot clothHumanoids drop it. No gathering profession needed, so your second slot stays free.
Craft what the route saysThe item is a means to a skill point, not gear. Below 125 most of it is plain white.
Bolt it before you bin itBolts are worth about four times the Merchant's Favor per piece of cloth that raw cloth is, and they give skill-ups on the way past.

What to do with the junk you craft

Most of the route below 125 makes plain white items nobody wants. Forever gives that output sinks Classic never had, and one of them should change what you craft.

bolts
Put bolts in a Fabrics crate, never cloth in a Textiles crate. A Journeyman Fabrics crate takes 6 Bolt of Woolen Cloth and pays 20 Merchant's Favor. A Journeyman Textiles crate takes 40 Wool Cloth and pays 10. Those six bolts cost you 18 wool, so bolting it first is worth roughly four times the Favor per piece of cloth, and the bolts give tailoring skill-ups on the way past. Turn crates in to Marcy Baker at Three Corners, western Redridge Mountains, around 9.7, 70.9.
writs
Keep one of each route item. Craftsman's Writs drop from mobs and chests, and each asks for one named crafted item. Two of them, Azure Silk Hood and Lesser Wizard's Robe, are items you already craft at 125–139; later ones want Small Silk Pack, Black Mageweave Vest and Mageweave Bag. A writ pays 75 reputation at Journeyman, 125 at Expert and 200 at Artisan plus a coin pouch, each distinct writ once a day, and you don't need the profession to hand one in.
woolen capes
Vendor them. No crate and no writ takes a Woolen Cape, and nothing confirms it can be disenchanted. Sell them and move on: the skill point was the product, the cape is a receipt.

Trainers worth walking to

Sources disagree on which Darnassus tailor teaches which rank, so check the trainer window rather than trusting any one list. Darnassus is free to reach, so check there first.

The route, 1 to 300

Materials are per craft. Patterns come from your trainer unless a vendor is named. Thread and dye come from any tailoring supply vendor. Type a skill level above and the row you are on is highlighted.

Two places to deviate. Robe of Power at 160 wants eight elementals per two points, which is the opposite of cheap — if elemental prices are bad on your realm, run Crimson Silk Vest and Crimson Silk Pantaloons through 150–190 instead. And everything from 234 up comes from a different source than the trainer line, so check what your realm's auction house actually charges for Ironweb Spider Silk before committing to the Cloak and Boots.
Treat 225 upward as a sketch. Method publishes a 225–300 route and then says of it: Leveling from 225 to 300 was untested in the WoW Forever Beta. We are unsure what recipes will be available and where you will be able to get them. The route above that line is agreed on by four sources; below it, check the trainer window before you buy materials.
