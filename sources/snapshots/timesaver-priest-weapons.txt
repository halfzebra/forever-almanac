Priest weapons in WoW Forever? Four changes matter. They come from Blizzard&#39;s Priest and Warrior
class deep dive , the WoW Forever development notes thread and the November 17 hotfixes . Wands lost
their spell damage scaling. Blizzard&#39;s beta development notes list this under Items: &quot;Wands:
Wands no longer gain any additional damage from the user&#39;s spell damage.&quot; (Blizzard, WoW Forever
Beta development notes) So a wand only does its own damage. The spell damage line printed on some wands,
such as +6 Shadow spell damage on Gravestone Scepter, still adds to your spells, but it is small. Choose
a wand for DPS and get your spell damage from the main hand and off-hand. Wands are slower to start.
The October 15 update notes say wands &quot;now require you to stand still for 0.5 seconds before shooting,
matching early versions of World of Warcraft.&quot; Stop moving before you start wanding a mob that is
already low. Wand Specialization is cheap. Blizzard&#39;s Priest deep dive says the Discipline talent
&quot;now only requires two talent points for the same benefit.&quot; The launch tree in our Priest talent
calculator shows 13% more wand damage at rank 1 and 25% at rank 2 . In our calculator, Maxroll&#39;s
Priest path levels as Discipline from 10 to 39 with Wand Specialization as the first two points, then
respecs to Shadow at 40 and buys it again at 41 and 42. The Icy Veins Holy leveling build also takes
it first. A Priest uses the wand on almost every mob, so take it early whichever tree you start in. Shadow-only
damage matters more now. Shadowform in the launch tree increases your Shadow damage by 10%, cuts the
Mana cost of Shadow spells by 50% and doubles the critical strike damage bonus of Shadow spells. Shadow
Word: Death is baseline at level 32 and Devouring Plague is available to every Priest at 20. Most of
your leveling damage is Shadow, so items that say &quot;Shadow spells and effects&quot; are nearly as
good as general spell damage for a Shadow Priest. They do nothing for Smite or Holy Fire, though. Blizzard
also warned about using low ranks. The October 1 notes say spells cast at ranks &quot;vastly below your
current level receive less benefit from your Spell Damage and Spell Healing.&quot; Spell damage gear
is worth the most when you keep your spell ranks trained. One Shadow change this week does not affect
leveling. The November 17 hotfix lowered Vampiric Embrace healing to 10% of the Shadow damage the Priest
deals (was 20%), and doubled the healing the casting Priest gets from it. Blizzard&#39;s note: &quot;This
change results in no difference for Priest solo play.&quot; What is the best Priest weapon at each level?
