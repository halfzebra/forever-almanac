19; of those that go on, 22.5% hold levels 20 to 29. Where does this Rogue leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Rogue calculator, which records the order every point was
placed in. 53,905 Rogue builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep
and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most
builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where
fewer than 25 agree. Can I follow this Rogue order in game? Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows
