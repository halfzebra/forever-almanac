Rogue weapons in WoW Forever? Four things, from Blizzard&#39;s Rogue and Warlock class deep dive (October
7, 2026) and the November 3 launch class notes . Your off hand gets its own button. The launch notes
added Shank , learned at level 24: &quot;Instantly strike with your off-hand weapon, dealing 50% weapon
damage and generating 15 Energy. Shank cannot Miss.&quot; It costs 5 Energy on a 10 second cooldown,
and rank 2 at level 44 generates 25 Energy. In Classic, the off hand mostly mattered for white swings
and the poisons they applied. In Forever, the off hand&#39;s weapon damage also feeds a special attack
every 10 seconds. Rogues can use one-handed axes. Blizzard explained the change in the deep dive: &quot;Rogues
as an archetype include brigands and outlaws, who often make use of axes in fantasy and medieval storytelling.&quot;
(Blizzard) The weapon specialization talents are now one talent. Dagger, Sword, Mace and Fist Weapon
Specialization were folded into Hack and Slash , row 5 of Combat. At 5 out of 5 points, the tooltip in
our Combat talent calculator reads: axes and swords get a 5% chance for an extra attack , daggers and
fist weapons get 5% critical strike chance , and maces ignore 15% of the target&#39;s armor . Maces no
longer stun. Poisons got stronger and stack with stones. Poison charges increased, and poisons can now
sit on a weapon together with a Sharpening Stone or Weightstone. The launch notes also doubled the Attack
Power scaling of Deadly Poison (0.009, was 0.0045) and Instant Poison (0.01, was 0.005). What did the
November 16 hotfix change for Rogues? The latest post in Blizzard&#39;s WoW: Forever hotfix thread went
up on November 17 at 02:17 UTC and covers the November 16 fixes. Under Rogue, Blizzard lists: &quot;Rogue
Poison ranks can no longer go above the maximum rank. Fixed an issue where using Shank on a target that
didn&#39;t have your combo points active on them would remove your combo points on other targets, even
if Boot Knife wasn&#39;t talented.&quot; (Kaivax, Blizzard) The same post fixed Mutilate getting an extra
attempt to proc Windfury Totem . The November 13 post had already clarified that off-hand strikes from
Mutilate do not activate Windfury Totem, while its main-hand strike still can. Earlier Rogue lines were
tooltip-only: Shank now shows Rank 1 and Rank 2, and shows the right combo points with Boot Knife (November
6). None of these changes which weapon is best, but the Shank fix matters if you switch targets often
while questing. What is the best Rogue dagger at each level? Assassination and Subtlety share the same
main-hand picks in our Assassination leveling gear planner . Backstab still needs a dagger in the main
hand, and the talent tooltips in our calculator show both Hemorrhage (145% weapon damage with a dagger,
100% without) and Ghostly Strike (180% with a main-hand dagger, 125% without) paying more when you hold
