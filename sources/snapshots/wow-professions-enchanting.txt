WoW Forever Enchanting Leveling Guide 1-300 This WoW Forever Enchanting leveling guide will show you
the fastest and cheapest way to level your Enchanting skill from 1 to 300. You will also find every trainer
location and where to train Enchanting past 75, 150 and 225. Enchanting goes well with Tailoring because
you don't need a gathering profession for either of them, and you can disenchant the items you made with
Tailoring. Below you will find the shopping list, the Enchanting trainers by starting zone and capital
city, and the cheapest enchants for every skill range. Forever added a few new low level recipes, so
the first 70 points are cheaper than in Classic. Shopping List These numbers are estimates. Once a recipe

Forever Disenchant is not in your spellbook , it's in the Enchanting box of the Professions window. My
disenchanting guide has the disenchanting table, and it shows with pictures where the Disenchant button
is: WoW Forever Disenchanting Guide Apprentice Enchanting (1-70) 1-2 1x Runed Copper Rod - 1 Copper Rod,
1 Mote of Magic The Copper Rod and the Mote of Magic are sold by the Enchanting Supply vendor near your
trainer, don't buy them from the Auction House. If you are leveling in a starting zone with a low level
character , you can buy the Copper Rod from a trade vendor there, but the Mote of Magic is only sold
by Enchanting vendors. In the starting zones only the trainers on Zephras Isle and in Tirisfal Glades
sell it, so everyone else has to buy it from the Enchanting Supply vendor next to the trainer in a capital
city. 2-21 19x Enchant Bracer - Inferior Stamina - 19 Mote of Magic Disenchanting gives skill points

you up to 225. To train Enchanting past 225 you need level 35 , then learn Artisan Enchanting from Annora
in Uldaman (I explain below how to find her). She is the only trainer who teaches Artisan Enchanting.
There are a few recipes only Annora teaches, so bring enough gold to learn every recipe and enough materials
to reach Enchanting 250 , otherwise you have to go back to Uldaman for the higher recipes. In total,
you will need around 12 gold to learn everything from Annora, and around 50 Vision Dust gets you to 250.
How to enter Uldaman Uldaman is in the Badlands and it has two entrances, use the back entrance. You
can't solo it at level 35, so go with a party or ask a level 60 to help. How to find Annora Annora only
spawns once you kill every mob in the area she is in! Don't forget to learn everything from Annora before
you leave Uldaman! The recipes from 230 up are taught nowhere else. Recipe Skill Cost Artisan Enchanting
