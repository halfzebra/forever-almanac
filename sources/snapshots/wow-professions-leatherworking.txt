WoW Forever Leatherworking Leveling Guide 1-300 This WoW Forever Leatherworking leveling guide will show
you the fastest and cheapest way to level your Leatherworking skill from 1 to 300. You will also find
every trainer location and where to train Leatherworking past 75, 150 and 225. Below you will find the
full shopping list, the Leatherworking trainers by starting zone and capital city, and the cheapest recipes
for every skill range from Apprentice to Artisan. Leatherworking is best combined with Skinning because
leather is most of the cost, but you can also buy everything in the guide from the Auction House. Check
out my WoW Forever Skinning leveling guide if you want to level Skinning too. Shopping List These numbers

22x Nightscape Headband - 110 Thick Leather, 44 Silken Thread Leatherworking Specializations Once you
reach Leatherworking 225 and level 40 , you can choose one of the three Leatherworking specializations:
