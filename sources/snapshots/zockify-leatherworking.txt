Skinning is the best profession to pair with Leatherworking in WoW Forever because most Leatherworking
patterns require leather, hides, or scales obtained through Skinning. If you can get your leather from
another source, such as an alt character or the Auction House, you can combine Leatherworking with any
other primary profession you like. Which Classes Benefit Most From Leatherworking in WoW Forever? Leatherworking
is useful for Druids and Rogues , since many Leatherworking patterns create leather armor. Hunters and
Shamans can also benefit from the profession&#8217;s mail armor recipes. For these 4 classes, Leatherworking
is already strong during the early levels. In WoW Forever, Leatherworking has multiple new patterns to
craft rare BoP items for every spec during the leveling journey, like Brawler&#039;s Leather Armor ,
Defender&#039;s Leather Pants , Trapper&#039;s Leather Helm , and many more. For the really powerful
items you need Merchant&#039;s Favor to buy the recipes on that character. Leatherworking Specializations
in WoW Forever WoW Forever retains the three classic Leatherworking specializations: Dragonscale Leatherworking
, Elemental Leatherworking , and Tribal Leatherworking . Dragonscale Leatherworking: Specializes in dragonscale
mail armor. Elemental Leatherworking: Specializes in leather armor made with elemental materials. Tribal
Leatherworking: Specializes in tribal leather armor. In Classic, these specializations require level
40 and 225 Leatherworking. Craftsman&#8217;s Writs for Leatherworking in WoW Forever Craftsman&#8217;s
Writs are random crafting orders obtained from Sealed Writs that drop from enemies throughout the world.
Opening a Sealed Writ (e.g. Sealed Journeyman&#039;s Writ ) gives you a random crafting order of its
rank. Leatherworking items are required for Craftsman&#8217;s Writ quests such as Craftsman&#039;s Writ:
Fine Leather Pants , Craftsman&#039;s Writ: Thick Leather Ammo Pouch , or Craftsman&#039;s Writ: Runic
Leather Headband . Obtain the requested item and deliver it, along with the Writ, to the NPC marked on
your map. The turn-in NPC varies by Writ. Writs are transferable, so you do not need to craft the requested
item yourself. Completing a Writ rewards reputation with the Azeroth Commerce Authority or Durotar Supply
and Logistics: Journeyman: 75 reputation Expert: 125 reputation Artisan: 200 reputation All Craftsman&#8217;s
