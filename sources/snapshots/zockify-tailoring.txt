Which Classes Benefit Most From Tailoring in WoW Forever? Tailoring is especially useful for Mages ,
Priests , and Warlocks , since Tailoring provides a large selection of cloth armor throughout the leveling
and endgame progression. Craftsman&#8217;s Writs for Tailoring in WoW Forever Craftsman&#8217;s Writs
are random crafting orders obtained from Sealed Writs that drop from enemies throughout the world. Opening
a Sealed Writ (e.g. Sealed Journeyman&#039;s Writ ) gives you a random crafting order of its rank. Tailoring
items are required for Craftsman&#8217;s Writ quests such as Craftsman&#039;s Writ: Lesser Wizard&#039;s
Robe , Craftsman&#039;s Writ: Azure Silk Hood , or Craftsman&#039;s Writ: Shadoweave Robe . Obtain the
requested item and deliver it, along with the Writ, to the NPC marked on your map. The turn-in NPC varies
by Writ. Writs are transferable, so you do not need to craft the requested item yourself. Completing
