and How to Play Levels 1–24: three simple habits Before a pull, spend spare Mana on Power Word: Shield
and Renew. Open by putting Shadow Word: Pain on as many mobs as you can safely handle, then wand them
down. Hard-casting Smite here is wasted Mana, whereas your wand is free and never interrupts your regeneration.
From 25: multiple targets are faster Spread Shadow Word: Pain across two or three targets, and drop Devouring
Plague plus Vampiric Embrace on the toughest one. Every kill triggers Spirit Tap and tops your Mana back
up, making multi-target pulls faster than fighting one mob at a time. If things crowd in on you, hit
Psychic Scream before trying to heal. Skip the long heal When your health dips to half, refresh your
shield, toss Renew, and keep going. Stopping for a long heal breaks your Mana regeneration—and once you
hit 42, it knocks you right out of Shadowform too. Priest Talents, Spells, and Gear by Level Each range

Gear and Stats Wand first Until Shadowform kicks in, your wand makes up a large chunk of your damage
— upgrade it every chance you get. Once you hit 42, spell damage matters more. Stats Prioritize Spirit
and Intellect while leveling: Spirit Tap doubles your Spirit on kills, and Meditation keeps half your
regeneration rolling while you cast. Spell damage and Stamina come next. Armor Priests wear cloth all
the way to 60, so Stamina on your cloth pieces is what keeps you upright when a pull goes sideways. Priest
Leveling Questions What is the best Priest leveling spec in WoW Forever? Shadow paired with a wand. This
build finishes at 31 Shadow, 17 Discipline, and 3 Holy, hits Shadowform at 42, and never needs a respec.
When does a Priest get Shadowform? Shadowform caps the Shadow tree, so level 42 is the earliest you can
unlock it. When does a Priest get Mind Flay? Mind Flay is a Shadow talent, and this build grabs it at
level 24. Should a Priest level with a wand? Yes. Wand Specialization boosts wand damage by 25%, wanding
spends zero Mana, and it never interrupts your Mana regeneration. It stays your main damage tool right
up to Shadowform. Can a Shadow Priest heal dungeons while leveling? In normal leveling dungeons, yes
— Flash Heal and Renew get the job done. From 42 onward you have to leave Shadowform to heal, so a dedicated
healer is better off in Discipline or Holy. How do I stop running out of Mana as Shadow? Play around
Spirit Tap: finish monsters with your wand, and fight several at once so each kill tops your Mana back
up. From 42, Shadowform slashes every Shadow spell&#39;s cost in half.
