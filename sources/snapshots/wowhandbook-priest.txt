and How to Play Levels 1–24: three things Before a pull, use spare Mana on Power Word: Shield and Renew.
Open with Shadow Word: Pain on as many monsters as you can safely handle. Then wand them down: casting
Smite at this stage is a waste, while the wand costs no Mana and does not interrupt your regeneration.
From 25: several targets are faster Put Shadow Word: Pain on two or three monsters and Devouring Plague
and Vampiric Embrace on the toughest one. Every kill triggers Spirit Tap and refills your Mana, so fighting
several monsters is faster than fighting one at a time. If you are surrounded, use Psychic Scream before
you try to heal. Avoid the long heal When your health drops to half, shield, Renew, and keep fighting.
A long heal breaks your Mana regeneration, and from 42 it also drops you out of Shadowform. Priest Talents,

Wand first Until Shadowform your wand is a large part of your damage, so upgrade it whenever you can.
After 42, spell damage matters more. Stats Spirit and Intellect while leveling: Spirit Tap doubles your
Spirit after kills, and Meditation keeps half your regeneration going while casting. Spell damage and
Stamina next. Armor Priests wear cloth the whole way; Stamina on cloth keeps you alive when a pull goes
wrong. Priest Leveling Questions What is the best Priest leveling spec in WoW Forever? Shadow with a
wand. The plan on this page ends at 31 Shadow, 17 Discipline, and 3 Holy, reaches Shadowform at 42, and
never needs a respec. When does a Priest get Shadowform? Shadowform is the last Shadow talent, so the
earliest you can take it is level 42. When does a Priest get Mind Flay? Mind Flay is a Shadow talent;
this plan takes it at level 24. Should a Priest level with a wand? Yes. Wand Specialization makes your
wand hit 25% harder, it costs no Mana, and it does not interrupt your Mana regeneration. It is your main
damage until Shadowform. Can a Shadow Priest heal dungeons while leveling? In normal leveling dungeons,
yes, with Flash Heal and Renew. You have to leave Shadowform to heal from 42, so a dedicated healer is
better served by a Discipline or Holy build. How do I stop running out of Mana as Shadow? Let Spirit
Tap trigger: kill monsters with your wand, and fight several at once so each kill refills your Mana.
From 42, Shadowform halves the cost of every Shadow spell.
