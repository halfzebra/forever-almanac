Sinister Strike is the button you press most, so the plan starts by making it cheaper: Efficient Strikes
cuts its cost by 5 Energy, and Remorseless Attacks gives your next Sinister Strike 40% more critical
strike chance after every kill. When you chain monsters, that bonus is almost always active. The Combat
tree then adds damage and Energy in layers. Coup de Grace and Aggression make Eviscerate hit much harder,
Riposte at 22 turns every parry into a cheap, strong strike, and Composure doubles the Energy saved by
Poised on your first attack. Flash of Steel at 37–41 is where the build feels different: your Shank generates
15 more Energy and comes back 2 seconds sooner, so it pays for itself. Assassination waits until Combat
is done because none of its talents are active abilities. All sixteen points are numbers: Malice for
critical strike, Improved Slice and Dice for longer uptime, Vigor for more Energy, Lethality for bigger
Sinister Strike critical strikes, and Cold Blood to finish. Combat Leveling: Point Order by Level Level

pick the main hand with the highest damage, since Sinister Strike scales with it. The off hand can be
any type. Stats Agility first, for Attack Power, critical strike, and dodge, then Strength, then Stamina.
Hit is covered by Precision. Armor Rogues wear leather the whole way. Agility leather is your upgrade
at every level. Rogue Leveling Questions What is the best Rogue leveling spec in WoW Forever? Combat
with Sinister Strike. The plan on this page ends at 35 Combat and 16 Assassination, takes Riposte at
22 and Adrenaline Rush at 42, and never needs a respec. Should a leveling Rogue use daggers or swords?
Swords, maces, axes, or fist weapons with Sinister Strike are smoother for leveling. Daggers suit Backstab
builds, which spend much of their time behind the target. When do Rogues get poisons? Instant Poison
and Crippling Poison at level 20, Deadly Poison at 30, and Wound Poison at 32. When do Rogues get Blade
Flurry and Adrenaline Rush? In this plan, Blade Flurry at 32 and Adrenaline Rush at 42. Save both for
packs and dungeon bosses. When do Rogues get Vanish and Kidney Shot? Vanish at level 22 and Kidney Shot
at level 30. How do I stop running out of Energy? Efficient Strikes makes Sinister Strike 5 Energy cheaper,
Composure makes your first attack of each fight far cheaper, and Flash of Steel and Adrenaline Rush add
Energy later. Use Riposte whenever it lights up: it costs very little.
