Sinister Strike on every cooldown, finish with Eviscerate or Slice and Dice, and fire back with Riposte
every time you parry. This build puts every point into Combat through 46, then closes out in Assassination
for critical strike, extra Energy, and Cold Blood — with zero respecs along the way. Rogue Leveling Milestones
Lv 4 Backstab and Pick Pocket. Lv 6 Gouge. Lv 8 Evasion — your emergency defense. Lv 10 Sap, Slice and
Dice, Sprint, and your first talent point. Lv 12 Kick. Lv 20 Instant Poison, Crippling Poison, and Rupture.
Lv 22 Vanish, plus Riposte for the Combat build. Lv 26 Cheap Shot. Lv 30 Deadly Poison and Kidney Shot.
Lv 42 Adrenaline Rush: doubles your Energy regeneration for 15 seconds. Rogue Combat Leveling Build Every
point goes into Combat until 46: cheaper Sinister Strike, harder-hitting Eviscerate, hit, Parry and Riposte,
followed by Blade Flurry, Flash of Steel, and Adrenaline Rush. From 47 onward, the final 16 points swing
into Assassination for critical strike, longer Slice and Dice, extra Energy, and Cold Blood. How the

35 / 0 Open Combat Leveling: Rotation and How to Play Rhythm Hit Sinister Strike whenever you have the
Energy. Finish a nearly dead mob with Eviscerate; if you are about to pull the next one, spend those
points on Slice and Dice instead so the buff rolls straight into the next fight. Press Riposte the instant
it lights up—it is cheap and hits hard. Weapons Swords, maces, axes, and fist weapons all get the job
done. Your off hand can be any weapon type, since Dual Wield Specialization simply boosts off-hand damage.
Hold Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush for multi-mob packs and dungeon bosses. Before level 10 Swap out
your starting dagger for any harder-hitting sword, mace, axe, or fist weapon the first chance you get.

Stats Weapons first Combat works with swords, maces, axes, and fist weapons alike. Put the hardest-hitting
weapon in your main hand since Sinister Strike scales with it; your off hand can be any type. Stats Agility
comes first for Attack Power, critical strike, and dodge, followed by Strength and then Stamina. Precision
takes care of your hit. Armor Rogues stay in leather from 1 to 60 — Agility leather is your upgrade at
every level. Rogue Leveling Questions What is the best Rogue leveling spec in WoW Forever? Combat built
around Sinister Strike. This build lands at 35 Combat and 16 Assassination, picks up Riposte at 22 and
Adrenaline Rush at 42, and never needs a respec. Should a leveling Rogue use daggers or swords? Swords,
maces, axes, or fist weapons paired with Sinister Strike make leveling smoother. Daggers suit Backstab
builds, which spend much of their time behind the target. When do Rogues get poisons? Instant Poison
and Crippling Poison unlock at level 20, Deadly Poison at 30, and Wound Poison at 32. When do Rogues
get Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush? In this build, Blade Flurry arrives at 32 and Adrenaline Rush at
42. Save both for packs and dungeon bosses. When do Rogues get Vanish and Kidney Shot? Vanish unlocks
at level 22, and Kidney Shot arrives at level 30. How do I stop running out of Energy? Efficient Strikes
shaves 5 Energy off Sinister Strike, Composure makes your first attack of each fight far cheaper, and
Flash of Steel plus Adrenaline Rush add extra Energy later on. Hit Riposte every time it lights up —
it costs very little.
