WoW Forever Tailoring Leveling Guide 1-300 This WoW Forever Tailoring leveling guide will show you the
fastest and cheapest way to level your Tailoring skill from 1 to 300. You will also find every trainer
location and where to train Tailoring past 75, 150 and 225. Below you will find the full shopping list,
the Tailoring trainers by starting zone and capital city, and the cheapest recipes for every skill range
from Apprentice to Artisan. Tailoring uses cloth as its main material, but cloth drops from humanoids,
so you don't need a gathering profession and you can combine Tailoring with anything. Enchanting is a
popular choice because you can disenchant the things you make while leveling and use the dust to level
Enchanting. Shopping List These numbers are estimates. Once a recipe turns yellow you won't get a skill
point from every craft, so you might need a few more or a few less. NOTE: I don't recommend buying all
the threads and dyes at once, because they fill up your bags. I list the ones you need at the start of
