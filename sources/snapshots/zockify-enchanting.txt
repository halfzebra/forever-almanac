in WoW Forever: New Formulas: WoW Forever adds 63 new Enchanting formulas, including staves such as Glimmering
Staff , wands such as Brilliant Wand , and new enchants for bracers and gloves. Use the filter &#8220;New
in Forever client data&#8221; in our WoW Forever Enchanting recipes database to see them. Weapon Enchants:
New melee weapon enchants such as Enchant Weapon - Revelation , Enchant Weapon - Recovery , and Enchant
Weapon - Insight trigger effects in combat, and two-handed weapons get new stat enchants. Necklace Enchants:
Enchant necklaces with bonuses such as Strength, Agility, Spell Power, Healing Power, or Deflection.
Off-Hand Enchants: New enchants include Enchant Off-Hand - Wisdom , which adds Intellect and Spirit to
an off-hand item. Craftable Class Relics: Craft new class relics, including Totems, Librams, and Idols
for Shamans, Paladins, and Druids. Waylaid Crates: Complete Waylaid Crates containing Enchanting materials
to earn Merchant&#8217;s Favor, which you can spend on profession recipes and other rewards. See our
guide on Waylaid Crates in WoW Forever for more information on how Waylaid Crates work and our guide
on Merchant&#8217;s Favor in WoW Forever for a list of all rewards you can purchase with the currency.
Legacy Points: Reach 150, 225, and 300 Enchanting to earn 1 Legacy Point at each milestone. See our guide
to the Legacy System in WoW Forever for more details. Profession Title: At 300 Enchanting, you can purchase
Enchanting Certification for 1000 Merchant&#039;s Favor . It permanently unlocks the account-wide &#8220;the
Enchanter&#8221; title. Characters need 300 Enchanting to display the title. Campsite Recipes: Tier 1:
Enchanted Lute Tier 2: Arcane Salvager Tier 3: Arcane Forge See our guide to the Camping System in WoW
Forever for more details. Weapon Enchants in WoW Forever WoW Forever adds three melee weapon enchants
that trigger an effect in combat. Each formula requires 140 Enchanting and costs 45 Merchant&#039;s Favor
: Enchant Weapon - Revelation : When one of your non-periodic spells does not critically strike, Revelation
can trigger and gives your next non-periodic spell 100% increased critical strike chance. The higher
your critical strike chance, the less often Revelation triggers. Enchant Weapon - Recovery : When an
enemy dodges or parries your attack, Recovery heals you for 5% of your maximum health. This can happen
at most once every 10 seconds. Enchant Weapon - Insight : Your spells have a chance to grant Insight,
which increases your Spirit by 100% for 10 seconds. Revelation suits spell damage dealers, while Recovery
helps melee characters and tanks stay alive. Insight suits healers and other mana users who regenerate
mana through Spirit. Two-Handed Weapon Enchants Two-handed weapons, including staves, also get new stat
enchants in WoW Forever: Enchant 2H Weapon - Lesser Agility : +15 Agility (Enchanting 210) Enchant 2H
Weapon - Lesser Strength : +15 Strength (Enchanting 220) Enchant 2H Weapon - Strength : +25 Strength
(Enchanting 290) Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Spell Power : up to +55 spell damage and healing (Enchanting
300) Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Healing Power : up to +100 healing and +34 spell damage (Enchanting 300)
Enchant 2H Weapon - Spellblasting : +65 Spell Power (Enchanting 300) The formulas for Enchant 2H Weapon
- Mighty Spell Power , Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Healing Power , and Enchant 2H Weapon - Spellblasting
cost 240 Merchant&#039;s Favor each. Necklace Enchants in WoW Forever WoW Forever adds permanent enchants

Strength : +5 Strength Enchant Necklace - Agility : +5 Agility Enchant Necklace - Spell Power : +6 Spell

. Off-Hand Enchants in WoW Forever WoW Forever includes three high-level enchants for off-hand items:
Enchant Off-Hand - Superior Intellect : +9 Intellect Enchant Off-Hand - Excellent Spirit : +12 Spirit
Enchant Off-Hand - Wisdom : +6 Intellect and +5 Spirit Formula: Enchant Off Hand - Wisdom is sold by
the Azeroth Commerce Authority and Durotar Supply and Logistics profession vendors for Merchant&#039;s
Favor and requires 300 Enchanting. The sources for Formula: Enchant Off Hand - Superior Intellect and
Formula: Enchant Off Hand - Excellent Spirit have not yet been confirmed for WoW Forever. Best Profession
to Pair With Enchanting in WoW Forever Tailoring is the best profession to pair with Enchanting in WoW
Forever. Neither profession requires a gathering profession, and cloth gear crafted with Tailoring can
be disenchanted into dust, essences, and shards used for Enchanting. You can also pick any other crafting
profession as your second profession, if you are able to gather the required materials for these professions
via an alt character or buy them in the Auction House. Which Classes Benefit Most From Enchanting in
WoW Forever? Enchanting can benefit every class in WoW Forever, but is generally more useful for casters
and healers. Enchanting also provides useful crafted items for several classes, including staves, wands,
off-hands, and class relics such as Totems, Librams, and Idols.
