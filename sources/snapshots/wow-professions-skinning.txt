WoW Forever Skinning Leveling Guide 1-300 This WoW Forever Skinning leveling guide will show you the
fastest way to level Skinning from 1 to 300. You will also find every trainer location and the best places
to skin for each skill range. This guide is aimed at higher level players who want to switch their profession,
but it's good for lower level players too. Leveling Skinning is easy but really boring, you will have
to run around and skin hundreds of mobs. Skinning goes well with Leatherworking because you can use the
leather you skin, so check out my Forever Leatherworking leveling guide if you want to level Leatherworking.

and Alliance on my WoW Forever Skinning Trainers page. Skinning Skill Requirements You need 5 times the
mob's level in Skinning to skin it, so a level 30 mob needs Skinning 150. If you follow the guide, you
don't have to worry about this. Mobs below level 20 need less, see the table below. Mob level Skinning
needed 1-10 1 11-19 10 for a level 11 mob, 10 more for every level (level 15 needs 50, level 19 needs
90) 20 and up 5 times the level If you enchant your gloves with Enchant Gloves - Skinning , you can skin
mobs 1 level higher than normal. Horde players can also pick the Keen Skinning Knife (+3 Skinning) as
a reward from the Unwelcome Guests quest in The Barrens. 1-75 In WoW Forever you can skin the mobs in
the starting areas, and they give skill points all the way up to 75. Any starting zone is good for this.
If you are leveling a new character, you can skin any mob in your starting zone, so you don't need a
recommendation until around level 10, when you leave the zone. The two places below are for players who
already have a higher level character and want to catch up on Skinning. A lot of players will be leveling
new characters there, so there is always something to skin. Don't forget to buy a Skinning Knife ! The
Leatherworking Supply vendors near your trainer and most General Goods vendors sell it. You don't have
to equip it, but you can't skin without one in your bags. Horde Alliance Skin the Mottled Boars in the
Valley of Trials in Durotar. Mob level: 1-2 Skin the Young Wolves and Timber Wolves in Northshire Valley
in Elwynn Forest. Mob level: 1-2 75-150 Visit your trainer and learn Journeyman Skinning if you haven't
yet, it requires Skinning 50. The Junior Skinning Trainers in the starting areas don't teach it. To train
past 150, visit your trainer again and learn Expert Skinning when you reach 125. Horde Alliance Kill
and skin Sunscale Scytheclaw , Zhevra Charger , Ornery Plainstrider . Go to the small raptor camp just
west of Northwatch Hold. Skin the level 16-17 ones until 80, from 80 you can skin the level 18 ones too.
Stay here until 150, the last 10 points are green but the respawn is fast. Mob level: 16-18 75-130 Loch
Modan Kill and skin Elder Mountain Boar , Black Bear Patriarch . Loch Crocolisk are also skinnable, make
sure to pull them when you get to the shore. Stay here until 130, you can't skin most of the Wetlands
raptors before that. Mob level: 16-17 130-150 Wetlands Kill and skin Mottled Scytheclaw , Mottled Razormaw
. There are always a few of them at Whelgar's Excavation Site, so you never run out of mobs to kill.
Mob level: 25-27 150-225 From 150, go to the yeti cave in Hillsbrad Foothills or to the Rotting Orchard
in Duskwood. Hillsbrad Foothills Duskwood Both factions can get there, Horde from Tarren Mill and Alliance
from Southshore. 150-160 In front of the cave Kill and skin Vicious Gray Bear , Starving Mountain Lion
. Stay here until 160 , at 150 you can only skin the level 30 Cave Yetis inside. Mob level: 22-24 160-225
Foothill Caverns Kill and skin Cave Yeti , Ferocious Yeti . The yetis respawn so fast that you can stay
inside the cave the whole time. Mob level: 30-32 Duskwood is an Alliance zone, but Grom'gol is close,
so Horde players can get there too. Kill and skin Nightbane Worgen , Nightbane Shadow Weaver , Nightbane
Dark Runner . Start at the area marked with 1, clear the mobs outside and inside Roland's Doom cave,
then go to the Rotting Orchard until the mobs respawn. Mob level: 26-29 225-280 Visit your trainer and
learn Artisan Skinning, it requires Skinning 200. Mount up and go to the yeti cave at Feral Scar Vale
in the south of Feralas. Both factions can get there, Horde from Camp Mojache and Alliance from Feathermoon
Stronghold. Kill and skin Feral Scar Yeti , Enraged Feral Scar , Hulking Feral Scar . At 225 you can't
skin the level 46 Hulking Feral Scars yet, but from 230 you can. Mob level: 43-46 280-300 From 280, fly
to Marshal's Refuge and skin the dinosaurs in the western part of Un'Goro Crater. Kill and skin Diemetradon
